Vancouver Regulation Toolkit

ARCH 540 Designing the Design · UBC SALA · Fall 2026 · Assignment 1, reviewed October 2, 2026

Assignment 1 asked each student to take one piece of a Vancouver regulation or design guideline, understand it, and build a small interactive tool with Claude that explains, computes and visualizes it. Four weeks, one tool per student. Each entry below has the student’s own one-sentence brief, a link to the tool, the live demo from the review, and the discussion that followed.
